Our story

Food treated with love and care

Coffe Spot started with a simple idea: coffee tastes better when it's treated like food. That means fresh roasting, seasonal menus, and a team that actually knows your name. And after a lot of love and patience we have managed to serve the best coffee in the area, serve pastries baked each morning, and give an environment you'd surely want to come to again.

What guides us

Our Values

Made with care

From our house-roasted blends to our morning pastries, everything is crafted in small batches.

Sourced with care

We buy directly from small farms and co-ops, paying fair prices for exceptional beans.

Built for community

The shop is designed to be a third place — somewhere between work and home.

Quality over hype

No trendy gimmicks. Just clean extraction, fresh ingredients, and consistent hospitality.
